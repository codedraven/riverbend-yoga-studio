About

Our Story

Founded in 2018 to make yoga welcoming for beginners and engaging for experienced yogis alike. We believe that yoga is a powerful tool for personal growth and well-being. We strive to offer a safe and supportive environment for everyone who wants to learn.

Meet Our Instructors

Maya Rivera: Studio Owner

Maya Rivera is the founder of Riverbend Yoga Studio and certified yoga instructor with over 10 years of experience. She has a passion for helping people.

John Smith: Yoga Teacher

John Smith is a certified yoga instructor with over 5 years of experience. He started his yoga journey to help with stress and anxiety and now he helps other people do the same.
